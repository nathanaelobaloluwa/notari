Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

NOTARI ("we", "us", or "the notice board") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we store it, who can access it, and how identification images submitted during admin signup are used.

1. What data we collect

  • Event registration details: the name and email you provide when reserving a seat for a campus event.
  • Admin signup details: username, email, club/organization name, password (stored only as a salted hash) and an identification image uploaded to request admin access.

2. How information is stored

All event registrations, admin accounts, and uploaded images are stored in a password-protected MongoDB Atlas cluster hosted in the cloud. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed with bcrypt before being written. Uploaded images are kept on the server's local filesystem under the uploads/ directory.

3. Who can access what

  • Identification images: viewable only by the superadmin, after authenticating, from the Access Requests dashboard in the Back Office.
  • Event attendee lists: visible only to the organizer who created that event (or a superadmin).
  • Student registration data: used solely to confirm your seat and issue your digital ticket pass. It is not shared with third parties.

4. Purpose of identification images

Identification images uploaded during an admin access request are collected solely for verifying eligibility for admin access and are reviewed only by the superadmin. They are not displayed publicly, not embedded in emails, and not shared with any other party.

5. Questions

Need to update or remove your data? Contact the superadmin through the Back Office or reach out to campus.events@univ.edu.
